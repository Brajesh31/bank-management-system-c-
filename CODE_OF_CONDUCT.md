# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on learning. Contributors are expected to use welcoming language, respect different experience levels, provide actionable technical feedback, and accept reasonable review comments.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainer through <https://github.com/Brajesh31>. For vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
